    Sensors

    What the on-board sensors measure. Move a slider and the app reads the new value over I2C, exactly as its driver would on the board.

    Plays a time-stamped sensor log into the sensors; the sliders keep your own settings meanwhile. Columns: t or t_ms, then any of temp, rh, lux, ax ay az, gx gy gz, mx my mz, pitch roll. Format

    Sound in the room

    Sound reaching the microphones: a test tone, plus how much of it each mic picks up. Mics are listed left to right as on the device.

    GPIO header inputs

    The expansion header, reached through the MAIN processor. The strip under the device shows what apps drive; here you drive pins from outside.

    Click a pin to drive it from outside: open → high → low. The SD card is held in memory and starts fresh on every load.

    RTT diagnostics

    The app's own log (DIAG() lines), as WiliBSP's fw rtt shows it on hardware. Grey lines are the emulator's notes.

  verify on hardware.
